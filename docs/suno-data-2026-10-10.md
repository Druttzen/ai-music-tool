# Suno data refresh — 2026-10-10

Product facts live in `app/lib/suno-current.js`. The installed reference panel
and draft export consume them. The current model family is v6: the regular and
wild variants require Pro/Premier; mini is available on Free. Earlier models
are retired. Standard creation returns two songs for 10 credits; Max Mode and
additional media can cost more. No unverified Max Mode price is supplied.

Sources: [v6 FAQ](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13924481) and
[release notes](https://www.suno.com/release-notes).

The [current plans page](https://suno.com/pricing) lists audio uploads up to
8 minutes on Free and 30 minutes on Pro/Premier. These supersede the shorter
limits in the older Upload Audio help article. Thirty seconds is the app's
local draft default, not a claimed platform upload maximum.

The app retains its 1000-character Style and 5000-character Lyrics copy budgets.
The reviewed official sources do not establish current v6 hard character caps,
so the UI no longer calls these values officially confirmed limits.

[Custom Models](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/11362497) use at least six
songs, with up to three private models for Pro/Premier. The
[Voices guide](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/11362369) accepts a 15-second
to 4-minute recording and up to a 2-minute selected excerpt. The separate
[My Taste guide](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/11362561) describes Suno's
personalization; this app's local history summary does not access that account
data.
